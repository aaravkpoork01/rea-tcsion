How it works

From registration to your exam in three steps

New candidates can register in a few minutes. Existing candidates can sign in straight away.

01

Enter your name, contact details and a photo, then choose or generate your 10-digit Login ID and set a password.

Create an account →

Before you begin

Keep these details ready

A smooth start makes a real difference. Check the points below before you register or sign in.

  • ✓Your own photo. Use a clear, recent passport-style photograph. It is shown to the administrator during approval and on your candidate panel.
  • ✓A working email or phone. The administrator may use it to reach you about your registration.
  • ✓A Login ID you will remember. It must be exactly 10 digits. You can type your own or generate a fresh one.
  • ✓A stable connection. If your session closes, sign in again to resume from your last saved answer and remaining time.
Please note

Approval is required before your first sign-in

After you submit the registration form, your account is marked pending. The administrator can approve or reject it after checking your details. Approval can take up to 24 hours.
